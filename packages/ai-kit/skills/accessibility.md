# Accessibility

Build accessible content and components by default.

- Use semantic landmarks and a logical heading sequence.
- Give links and buttons clear purpose; do not rely on color, position, or icon shape alone.
- Require useful alternatives for meaningful images, testimonials, and logo links.
- Keep keyboard focus visible and interaction reachable without a pointer.
- Associate form inputs with labels and expose validation errors to assistive technology.
- Check contrast and reduced-motion behavior in component CSS; these cannot be verified from content payloads alone.
- Report observed issues precisely and avoid claiming WCAG conformance without documented testing.
